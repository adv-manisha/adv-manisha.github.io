Legal Research & Working Papers

Rigorous scholarship interrogating capital market jurisprudence, SEBI statutory power, corporate governance standards, and interdisciplinary linguistic reform in commercial drafting.

Securities Law

SEBI's Regulatory Overreach: Analysing the Boundaries of Quasi-Judicial Power in Securities Adjudication

This paper examines the constitutional and statutory limits of SEBI's adjudicatory authority under the SEBI Act, 1992. It analyses landmark SAT and Supreme Court decisions to map the evolving contours of due process in securities enforcement, arguing for a clearer separation between SEBI's investigative and adjudicatory functions.

2024  |  LLM Dissertation Draft
Capital Markets

Disclosure Adequacy in Indian IPOs: A Critical Review of SEBI's ICDR Regulations

Through a comparative analysis of SEBI's Issue of Capital and Disclosure Requirements Regulations with SEC Rule S-K and EU Prospectus Regulation, this paper evaluates whether Indian IPO prospectus norms adequately protect retail investors. It proposes targeted amendments to improve risk factor specificity and financial statement accessibility.

2024  |  Seminar Paper
Corporate Governance

Independent Directors in India: Myth, Mandate, and the Post-Uday Kotak Committee Reality

Drawing on empirical data from NSE-listed companies, this paper interrogates whether SEBI's post-Kotak Committee reforms have meaningfully enhanced independent director efficacy. It identifies structural impediments — promoter influence, information asymmetry, and liability chilling — that undermine board independence in practice.

2023  |  Research Paper
M&A Law

Creeping Acquisitions and the SEBI Takeover Code: Plugging the Gaps in Threshold Regulation

This paper analyses how sophisticated acquirers exploit definitional ambiguities in the SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011 to avoid mandatory open offer obligations. It proposes a look-through approach modelled on UK Panel practice to address regulatory arbitrage in domestic M&A transactions.

2023  |  Working Paper
Law & Literature

The Language of Corporate Contracts: Plain-Language Reform and the Readability Crisis in Indian Commercial Drafting

Drawing on her background in English and Hindi literature, this paper examines how the opacity of commercial contract language perpetuates power asymmetries between sophisticated parties and retail investors or small businesses. It advocates for a plain-language standard in SEBI-mandated disclosures, benchmarked against readability reforms in UK and Australian financial regulation.

2023  |  Interdisciplinary Research Paper
Legal Linguistics

Rajbhasha and Corporate Compliance: Assessing the Effectiveness of Hindi in Statutory Filings under the Companies Act, 2013

This paper investigates the practical and constitutional dimensions of Hindi as a medium for corporate compliance documentation under the Companies Act, 2013 and SEBI regulations. It evaluates current bilingual filing norms, identifies gaps in translation quality and legal equivalence, and proposes a framework for more meaningful Rajbhasha compliance in capital market disclosures.

2022  |  LLB Research Paper
