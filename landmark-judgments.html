In Re: Section 6A of the Citizenship Act, 1955 (Assam Accord)
(2025) 2 SCC 1
2025
5-Judge Constitution Bench (4:1 Majority)
 Supreme Court Citizenship Act Sec 6A, Articles 11 & 14
The legislative classification underlying Section 6A satisfies the twin tests of Article 14: an intelligible differentia based on the historic influx during the 1971 Bangladesh Liberation War, and a direct rational nexus with the objective of restoring social peace in the border state of Assam.
Upheld the statutory validity of the Assam Accord cut-off date (25 March 1971).

2024

Overruled the 1989 landmark decision in India Cements v. State of Tamil Nadu. Overruled India Cements (1989) by holding that royalty is not tax.

2024
3-Judge Bench (D.Y. Chandrachud CJI, J.B. Pardiwala JJ.)

The phrase 'as soon as possible' in the first proviso to Article 200 conveys constitutional urgency. A Governor cannot derail the legislative process through inaction. If assent is withheld, the bill must be returned; upon repassing by the Assembly, the Governor has no option but to grant assent.
