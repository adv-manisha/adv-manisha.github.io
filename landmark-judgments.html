🏛️ Constitutional Law & Fundamental Rights

6 Landmark Rulings
🏛️ Supreme Court of India (Apex Court Precedents)
Case Title & Citation Bench & Year Forum Key Section / Article Core Ratio Decidendi Brief
Kesavananda Bharati v. State of Kerala
(1973) 4 SCC 225
1973
13-Judge Bench
 Supreme Court Article 368
Parliament possesses wide constituent power to amend any provision of the Constitution, but cannot alter or destroy its Basic Structure.
Overruled Golak Nath; solidified judicial review.
 📄 PDF
Justice K.S. Puttaswamy v. Union of India
(2017) 10 SCC 1
2017
9-Judge Bench
 Supreme Court Article 21 & Part III
The Right to Privacy is a fundamental right emanating from personal liberty under Article 21 and the entire constellation of Part III freedoms.
Established the 3-fold proportionality test.
 📄 PDF
Navtej Singh Johar v. Union of India
(2018) 10 SCC 1
2018
5-Judge Bench
 Supreme Court Articles 14, 15, 19, 21
Section 377 IPC, in so far as it penalizes consensual sexual activity between adults in private, is unconstitutional. Constitutional Morality supercedes popular morality.
Affirmed identity and equal dignity as non-negotiable.
 📄 PDF
Maneka Gandhi v. Union of India
(1978) 1 SCC 248
1978
7-Judge Bench
 Supreme Court Articles 14, 19, 21
Procedure depriving liberty under Article 21 cannot be arbitrary, fanciful or oppressive; it must be just, fair and reasonable. Part III forms an interrelated 'Golden Triangle'.
Imported substantive due process into Indian law.
 📄 PDF

2022 SCC OnLine Guj 1450
2022
Division Bench
 Gujarat HC Article 226 & 14
High Court writ review extends to procedural fairness and protection against arbitrary state action in public service and contractual matters.
Reiterated natural justice as an unwritten Article 14 command.

2009 SCC OnLine Del 1662
2009
Division Bench
 Delhi HC Articles 14, 15, 21

Groundbreaking foundation for privacy and LGBTQ+ rights.
