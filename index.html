01 / About & Leadership
MG
Adv. Manisha Gandhi Advocate · Co-Founder, Law Glimpse

Bridging Regulation
and Practice

Advocate Manisha Gandhi brings a rare, interdisciplinary perspective to commercial and securities jurisprudence. Combining an advanced humanistic background with rigorous legal training, she holds a Master of Laws (LLM) in Business and Corporate Law from Sabarmati University, an LLB from Gujarat University, and dual Master of Arts degrees in English Literature and Hindi from Mohanlal Sukhadia University.

Her legal practice and scholarship focus on the regulatory architecture governing Indian capital markets, SEBI enforcement boundaries, independent director accountability, and plain-language reform in commercial drafting. Her multidisciplinary mastery sharpens her ability to decode intricate statutory frameworks, construct persuasive pleadings, and draft transparent contracts.
